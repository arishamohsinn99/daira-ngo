About Daira

Some things become so familiar that we stop questioning them: silence around difficult conversations, shame around women's experiences, and limits girls are taught to accept as destiny.

Daira begins with a question: what happens when we refuse to accept these things as inevitable?

ہم بھی دیکھیں گے — "we will see too" — is a commitment to look courageously, question what has been hidden or denied, and believe that generations-old norms can change.

As a youth-led initiative, Daira creates spaces where women and girls can speak without shame, access knowledge and support, and claim their place in their communities. We make difficult conversations possible and essential resources accessible, not as charity, but as matters of dignity and justice.

We believe empowerment grows through access, solidarity, and the freedom to shape one's life. It begins with listening to those most affected and letting their voices guide our work.

Hum Bhi Dekhenge means seeing what becomes possible when silence is no longer mistaken for peace, and every woman and girl has the freedom, support, and confidence to be heard.
