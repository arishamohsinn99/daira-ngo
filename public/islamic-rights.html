Islamic Rights

Rights granted to women in Islam, in plain language — because they're often taught selectively, or not taught at all.

Marriage & family life

  • Right to consent — A marriage isn't valid without her clear agreement; no one can force her into it. (Sahih al-Bukhari 6946; Sahih Muslim 1419)
  • Right to Mahr — Her husband owes her a mahr (dower) at marriage. It's hers alone to keep, spend, or invest — never a family or household expense. (Quran 4:4)
  • Right to Khula — She can seek to end a marriage, generally by returning her mahr, even without her husband's agreement. (Quran 2:229)
  • Right to maintenance (Nafaqah) — Her husband is responsible for her food, clothing, and housing throughout the marriage. (Quran 2:233)
  • Right to kindness — She is entitled to fairness, patience, and respect within marriage and family life. (Quran 4:19)

Property & economic rights

  • Right to inherit — She inherits as a daughter, wife, mother, and sister, with a defined share that no one can take from her. (Quran 4:7, 4:11–12)
  • Right to own property — Anything she owns, earns, or is given is hers alone; she is never obligated to spend it on her household. (Quran 4:32)
  • Right to work & trade — She may earn, invest, and run her own business, and keep everything she earns. (reflected in the example of Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, a successful businesswoman)

Education & knowledge

  • Right to seek knowledge — Seeking knowledge is described as an obligation on every Muslim, male and female alike, with no ceiling on how far she can take it. (Sunan Ibn Majah 224)

Dignity, consent & protection

  • Right to refuse — She can say no to a marriage proposal; a guardian cannot marry her off against her will. (Sahih al-Bukhari 5138)
  • Right to bodily safety — No one — husband, father, or otherwise — has the right to harm her. (Quran 4:19)
  • Right to be heard — Her voice, her testimony, and her opinion count and must be considered. (Quran 2:282, on testimony in financial matters)

Spiritual standing

  • Equal worth before God — Men and women are held to the same standard of faith and accountability, with no difference in spiritual worth. (Quran 33:35)
References are given so you can look them up yourself, not as a substitute for scholarly guidance — translations and interpretations can vary between schools of thought.
